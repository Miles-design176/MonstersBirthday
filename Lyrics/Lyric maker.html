Preview settings

If you don't add audio, use Play and the timeline slider to preview the lyric timings against a simulated clock.

Enter-to-sync mode

Paste your lyrics below, one line per row. Start playback, then press Enter (or the button) when each line begins and press it again when that line ends. The tester will move to the next line automatically.

Paste your lyrics, then choose Start / reset sync.

Keyboard tip: Enter works while you're on this page, except when typing in a text field. You can also click the Mark start / Mark end button.

Lyrics and timestamps

Paste the lyrics in the same format used by your birthday page: [startSeconds, endSeconds, "line"]. Times are seconds from the start of the song.

You can also use the older two-part format [startSeconds, "line"]; its end time will be the next line's start.
